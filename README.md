# GST Assistant

## The problem
GST rates and rules in India change often, and every change brings new rates and new rules. The documents are huge, so it is hard for professionals to keep up and find the latest answer.

## Who it's for
CMA and CA professionals and students, accountants, and consultancies that provide services to companies.

## What it does
A user asks a GST question, for example "What is the latest GST rate on this service?" or "Who bears the tax?". The app gives the latest answer from official documents, with the source and its date.

## Scope
Starting with GST rates on goods and services.

## Disclaimer
This tool shares information from official documents. It is not professional tax advice.
